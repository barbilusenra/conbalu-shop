## Prompt exacto

Use case: ads-marketing. Create a single wide 3:2 image containing exactly THREE adjacent equally wide vertical editorial graphic design posters, edge to edge, no gutters, no perspective, no scene background or mockup. This will be sliced by CSS as three concept previews in a Canva template shop named Conbalu. Left third: bright pink #EA72AD poster, dark brown bold oversized modern grotesk typography reading "MAKE SOME NOISE", playful premium art direction, chrome sculptural asterisk in lower half, tiny typographic details. Middle third: pale butter yellow #F4ED89 poster, strong dark brown typography reading "GOOD THINGS TAKE SHAPE", a striking real photographic pink calla lily still life cutting across the middle, stylish independent magazine composition. Right third: dark brown #30281D poster with blush #F4D6E1 giant typography reading "LESS SCROLL MORE SOUL", expressive sculptural pale pink ribbon loop, small editorial accent text. Each poster must fill exactly one third and be a coherent separate portrait artwork. Ultra sharp graphic design, bold Swiss typography, tactile print grain, art school meets fashion editorial, typography readable and sophisticated. No UI, no website, no rounded cards, no shadows between posters, no people, no customer claims. Exact full-bleed thirds. Size landscape 1536x1024.
